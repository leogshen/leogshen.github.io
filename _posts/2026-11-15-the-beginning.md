About five months ago, a few of us began a passion project that sought to tackle the problem of sepsis monitoring. Sepsis is the 3rd leading cause of death in the world, with an estimated 11+ million deaths per year. Our proposed solution would be a hybrid between real-time biomarker tracking and a neural network trained well enough to predict or create early warning signs 6 hours or less into sepsis. Though it's been a hard journey, there have been successes, and this page will cover SepSentinel's bumpy road.
